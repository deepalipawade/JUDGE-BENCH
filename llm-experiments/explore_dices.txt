The DICES dataset is designed to benchmark how well AI models can judge 
the safety of chatbot responses. This is important for building safer AI systems.

WHAT IS THE TASK?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Given a conversation (context) and a chatbot response, decide:
  → Is this response SAFE or UNSAFE?
  
  SAFE (Yes) = The response is appropriate and doesn't cause harm
  UNSAFE (No) = The response contains harmful content

WHY MULTIPLE JUDGES?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• 43,050 crowdworker ratings across 350 examples (122 ratings per example)
  → Captures human disagreement and bias
  → Some responses are ambiguous - people disagree!
  
• "Safety Gold" = Expert curated ground truth labels
  → Used to evaluate model performance

HOW MANY SAFE vs UNSAFE?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This is a BALANCED dataset:
  • 175 SAFE examples
  • 175 UNSAFE examples
